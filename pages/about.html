About Khidma

Khidma Ladies Community Group is a welcoming space where women can connect, build friendships, gain confidence and take part in activities that strengthen the community.

Our Story

Khidma began as a small group of women coming together with a shared goal: creating a supportive space where women could connect, socialise and feel valued.

Our Journey

From humble beginnings, Khidma has grown into a community where women can share experiences, learn new skills and take part in activities that encourage connection and wellbeing.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive and supportive environment where women feel empowered, connected and part of a strong community.

Our Values

💜 Community | 💜 Support | 💜 Inclusion | 💜 Confidence | 💜 Friendship

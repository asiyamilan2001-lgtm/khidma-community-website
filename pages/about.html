About Khidma

Khidma Ladies Community Group is a welcoming community organisation dedicated to supporting women, building confidence and creating opportunities for connection, learning and wellbeing.

Our Mission

To create a safe and supportive environment where women can meet, develop skills, access support and strengthen community connections.

Our Vision

A connected community where every woman feels valued, supported and empowered.

Our Values

Respect, inclusion, kindness, empowerment and community support are at the heart of everything we do.

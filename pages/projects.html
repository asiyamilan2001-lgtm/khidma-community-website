Our Projects

Khidma Ladies Community Group supports women and the wider community through activities, projects and initiatives that encourage connection, learning and positive change.

Community Support

Creating a welcoming environment where women can meet, share experiences and support one another.

Health & Wellbeing

Encouraging wellbeing through community activities, awareness sessions and opportunities to connect.

Skills & Learning

Helping women develop confidence, learn new skills and explore personal development opportunities.

Khidma Ladies Recycle

Supporting environmental awareness and encouraging positive community action through recycling initiatives.

Community Events

Bringing women together through gatherings, workshops and activities that celebrate community connections.

Outreach & Connection

Helping create links within the community and making sure women have opportunities to feel involved and supported.

Working Together

Every project helps strengthen connections, encourage confidence and build a more supportive community.

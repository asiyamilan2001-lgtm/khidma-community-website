Our Projects

Khidma Ladies Community Group delivers projects and activities designed to support women, improve wellbeing and strengthen the local community.

Women's Support

Creating a welcoming space where women can connect, share experiences and access support.

Health & Wellbeing

Working with community partners to encourage healthier lifestyles through awareness sessions and wellbeing activities.

Skills & Opportunities

Helping women build confidence, learn new skills and discover volunteering and employment opportunities.

Community Events

Organising activities that bring people together, celebrate community and reduce social isolation.

Environmental Projects

Supporting initiatives that encourage sustainability and positive change within the local area.

Community Outreach

Reaching out to support people from different backgrounds and help create a stronger community.

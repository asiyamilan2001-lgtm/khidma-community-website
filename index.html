WELCOME TO KHIDMA

A place to connect,
learn and grow together.

Khidma Ladies Community Group is a welcoming community space where women can come together, build friendships, develop confidence and take part in activities that encourage connection, learning and wellbeing.

From creative activities and workshops to community events and opportunities to learn new skills, Khidma aims to create a supportive environment where every woman feels welcome and valued.
